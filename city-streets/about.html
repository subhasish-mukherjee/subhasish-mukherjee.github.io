Definitions for the statistics shown in the dashboard. Street, path, infrastructure, and land-use data come from OpenStreetMap; grades come from AWS Terrain Tiles. “—” means that a value is unavailable.

Area and population

StatisticDefinition
Windowdisc: a fixed-radius area around the city center. boundary: the municipal boundary.
Analyzed core areaArea of the retained urban core, in km². The core is the center-connected set of 400 m cells with at least 25 intersections/km²; enclosed sparse cells are included.
PopulationApproximate city-proper population from the city registry.
Land areaCity-proper land area, in km², from the same registry unit as population.
Population densityPopulation divided by city-proper land area, in people/km².
RegionGeographic region assigned in the city registry.
TerrainQualitative terrain category assigned in the city registry; it is not computed from the street network.

Direction and orientation

Bearings and grades are weighted by street-segment length. Opposite travel directions are equivalent.

StatisticDefinition
Bearing roseStreet-length share in each of 36 compass-bearing bins, mirrored by 180°.
H₂DShannon entropy of the bearing rose: −Σ pᵢ ln pᵢ. Lower values indicate concentration in fewer directions.
φ order1 − ((H₂D − ln 4)/(ln 36 − ln 4))². A single orthogonal grid approaches 1; a uniform bearing distribution approaches 0.
Grid angle θDominant grid rotation from the fourfold circular moment of the bearing distribution, reported in [0°, 90°).
Direction measureStreet-length distribution over bearing and grade angle on ℝP². The displayed equal-area band covers grades up to 50%; larger retained grades enter the top bin.
H sphereNormalized entropy of the binned bearing-by-grade direction measure. 0 means all mass is in one tile; 1 means uniform mass over the band.
Vertical fractionLength-weighted mean squared vertical component of street direction, T₃₃, displayed as a percentage.
Sphericityτ₃/τ₁, using the smallest and largest eigenvalues of the direction second-moment tensor. 0 is line-concentrated; 1 is isotropic.

Grade and hilliness

Grade is rise divided by horizontal run. Bridges, tunnels, segments shorter than about 15 m, and grades above 60% are excluded from grade statistics.

StatisticDefinition
HillinessLength-weighted mean absolute grade of the drivable street network, in percent.
>8% lengthShare of graded drivable-street length with absolute grade above 8%.
Walk hillinessLength-weighted mean absolute grade of the pedestrian network, including staircases, in percent.
Grade distributionShare of street length in each absolute-grade bin.
Hill fanThe grade distribution plotted at the corresponding inclination angle; radius is normalized to the largest bin.
Steepest named streetsHighest retained segment grade for each named street.

Staircases

A staircase is one connected component of OSM highway=steps segments. Grade uses a mapped step count at 0.17 m per step when available, otherwise terrain elevation.

StatisticDefinition
Staircases/kmConnected staircases per km of walkable street.
Major staircasesCount of connected staircases at least 30 m long.
Major stairs/kmMajor staircases per km of walkable street.
Major stairs/km²Major staircases per km² of analyzed core; this depends on the selected extent.
Mean staircase lengthMean length of connected staircases, in meters.

Street geometry and connectivity

StatisticDefinition
CircuityStreet length divided by straight-line endpoint distance. A straight segment has value 1.
Turn rateTotal change in direction along same-road chains, in degrees per 100 m. Ramps and roundabouts are excluded.
Curvy shareShare of street length on chains at least 100 m long with turn rate at least 15° per 100 m.
Mean block lengthMean length, in meters, of same-road chains between intersections.
Intersections/km²Street intersections divided by analyzed core area.
Street km/km²Total street length divided by analyzed core area.
4-way shareShare of intersections incident to four streets.
Dead-end shareShare of intersections incident to one street.
Mean streets/intersectionMean number of incident streets per intersection.
SegmentsNumber of analyzed network segments.
Street lengthTotal analyzed street length, in km.

Street infrastructure

StatisticDefinition
Signals/km²OSM highway=traffic_signals nodes divided by analyzed core area.
Signals/intersectionTraffic-signal nodes divided by street intersections.
One-way shareShare of drivable-street length tagged one-way.
Bridge shareShare of drivable-street length tagged as bridge.
Sidewalk shareShare of street length recorded with sidewalk provision in OSM.

Walk Score metrics

StatisticDefinition
Walk Score®Walk Score amenity-access index. City values are means over sampled core points; neighborhood values use the neighborhood center.
Bike Score®Walk Score bicycle-friendliness index, averaged over sampled core points when available.
Transit Score®Walk Score public-transit-access index, averaged over sampled core points when available.

Score definitions and availability are provided by Walk Score.

On-street parking

StatisticDefinition
Parking asphalt shareEstimated parking-lane area divided by total roadway area on streets with mapped parking. Assumed widths are 2.0 m for parallel parking, 4.3 m for diagonal parking, 5.0 m for perpendicular parking, and 3.0 m per travel lane.
Parking coverageShare of street length with usable OSM parking data.
Parking mapped kmStreet length with usable parking data. Parking asphalt share is shown only when this is at least 30 km.
Both-side parking shareShare of parking-mapped street length with parking on both sides.
One-side parking shareShare of parking-mapped street length with parking on one side.

Parks and green space

Areas are 20 m raster unions clipped to the analyzed core, so overlapping polygons are counted once.

StatisticDefinition
Park areaArea, in km², of OSM parks, nature reserves, commons, dog parks, gardens, recreation grounds, and village greens.
Park sharePark area divided by analyzed core area.
All green-space areaPark area plus mapped sports pitches, playgrounds, golf courses, forest, meadow, and amenity grass, in km².
All green-space shareAll green-space area divided by analyzed core area.

City similarity

StatisticDefinition
Sphere W₂Wasserstein-2 distance between bearing-by-grade measures on ℝP², minimized over horizontal rotation.
Grid W₂Circular Wasserstein-2 distance between bearing distributions, minimized over rotation; grade is ignored.
Elevation W₂One-dimensional Wasserstein-2 distance between grade-angle distributions; bearing is ignored.
JS divergenceJensen–Shannon divergence between direction measures, minimized over horizontal rotation.
Normalized distanceEach Wasserstein distance is divided by the diameter of its ground space; JS is divided by ln 2. Values lie in [0, 1].
MDS coordinatesTwo-dimensional multidimensional-scaling coordinates fitted to the selected distance matrix.

Neighborhoods

Neighborhood statistics use the same definitions, restricted to the registered circular neighborhood window.

Sources

Networks and land use: OpenStreetMap via Overpass. Elevation: AWS Terrain Tiles, sampled bilinearly. Basemap: Carto/OpenStreetMap. Orientation-order method: Boeing (2019). Distance calculations use circular and spherical optimal transport.
